WorkBook is CTDI’s invitation-only internal network. This guide shows how to join, how admins manage people and branches, and how to publish company news.

WorkBook runs in any web browser on a computer or phone. Your manager or HR sends you the web address together with your invitation.

Getting started

You can register only with the exact email address your invitation was sent to. Your role and branch come from the invitation automatically.

Create your account

  1. Open the WorkBook web address you received.
  2. Click Got an invitation? Create your account.
  3. Enter your full name, the invited work email and a password of at least 12 characters with letters and numbers.
  4. Click Create account. The screen shows Check your email.
  5. Open the confirmation email and click the link. Open it in the same browser where WorkBook is open.
  6. Return to WorkBook and sign in with your email and password.

Invitations expire after 14 days. If yours has expired, ask your manager or HR for a new one.

Sign in

  1. Enter your email and password, then click Sign in.
  2. If you have two-factor sign-in, enter the 6-digit code when asked.

Forgot your password

  1. On the sign-in screen click Forgot password? and enter your email.
  2. Open the email in the same browser and click the link.
  3. Choose a new password on the Set a new password screen.

For security, WorkBook shows the same message for a wrong email and a wrong password. It never confirms whether an account exists.

Two-factor sign-in for managers and admins

HR, managers and admins must turn on two-factor sign-in. Until they do, their account works like a regular employee’s: inviting, moderating, publishing news and admin actions are blocked.

Set up the authenticator

  1. Sign in and click the shield icon in the top-right corner, or Set up on the red banner.
  2. Click Set up authenticator app. A QR code appears.
  3. Scan it with your phone. iPhone: point the Camera at the QR code and tap Add Verification Code in Passwords (no app download needed). Android: use Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
  4. Type the 6-digit code from your phone into WorkBook. The screen shows Two-factor authentication is on.

After this, WorkBook asks for a fresh code each time you sign in.

Fingerprint or Windows Hello (optional)

Once the authenticator is on, you can click Add passkey on this device on the same screen. Next time, click Sign in with Windows Hello / fingerprint instead of typing a password and code.

  • Add passkeys only after the authenticator. A passkey added earlier does not unlock manager or admin actions, and the security screen shows a warning to remove it.
  • Passkeys work in the browser version only.

If you lose your phone, ask an admin. Never share your codes with anyone, including IT.

Admin panel

Open Admin in the left menu. It has four tabs: Roles & users, Structure, Access and Audit log. Two-factor sign-in must be on.

Invite people (Access tab)

  1. Click Invite next to Pending invitations.
  2. Enter the person’s Work email, pick a Role and a Branch.
  3. Click Create invitation, then tell the person to open WorkBook and click Got an invitation? within 14 days.

To cancel an invitation, remove it from the Pending invitations list. HR and managers can also invite from Directory → Invite a colleague.

You are You can invite as Into
HR Guest, Employee, Team lead Your own branch
Branch manager Guest, Employee, Team lead Your own branch
Regional manager Guest, Employee, Team lead Any branch in your region
Global admin Any role below global admin Any branch
Super admin Any role below super admin Any branch

Company email domains (super admin only): add a domain such as company.com to let anyone with that email register as a Guest without an invitation. Leave the list empty to keep registration invitation-only.

Change roles and branches (Roles & users tab)

Each person has a Role and a Branch dropdown. People marked Not yet assigned to a branch need a branch before they see branch content. You cannot give anyone a role equal to or higher than your own.

Regions and branches (Structure tab)

  • Click Region to add a region, or Add branch to [region] under a region.
  • Use the pencil icon to rename or edit a branch (address, phone, email), and the trash icon to delete.
  • A branch that still has employees, or a region that still has branches, cannot be deleted. Move people or branches first.

Remove a person who left

  1. Open the person’s profile and click Archive employee. Their access stops immediately.
  2. The super admin opens Moderation → Archive requests and either deletes the account permanently or restores it.

Audit log tab

Shows who did what and when: invitations, role changes, archiving, structure changes, moderation. Nobody can edit or erase it.

Company news

News is for official updates; the Feed is for everyday posts. Each news item goes to one audience, and only that audience can see it.

Audience Who sees it Who can publish
Global Everyone in the company Global admins, super admin
Region Every branch in one region Regional manager, HR of that region, admins
Branch One branch Branch manager, HR, regional manager of that region, admins

Publish a news item

  1. Open News in the left menu and click Write company news.
  2. Choose the audience: global, region or branch. Only the options your role allows are shown. HR and branch managers post to their own branch or region automatically.
  3. Pick a category: announcement, policy or event.
  4. Write a clear Title and the text.
  5. Optional: click Attach to add photos, one video or a PDF.
  6. Publish. Everyone in the audience gets a notification.

Edit or delete

Open the menu on your news item and choose Edit or Delete. Admins can remove any item.

Good practice

  • One topic per item; put the key fact or date in the title.
  • Use policy for rules people must follow and attach the PDF.
  • Choose the smallest audience that needs it: a branch notice should not go global.
  • Limit: 20 news items per hour per person.

Moderation and troubleshooting

Reported posts

Any employee can report a post. Branch and regional managers and admins open Moderation, review each report and choose remove or dismiss. Managers see reports for their own branch or region only.

Limits per person

Action Limit
Posts 5 a minute, 40 an hour
Comments 10 a minute, 200 an hour
Messages 30 a minute
File uploads 30 a minute, 500 a day
Invitations 50 a day

Files allowed: photos, video up to 50 MB, PDF up to 20 MB.

This action requires two-factor authentication Turn on two-factor sign-in (see above) and sign in again
